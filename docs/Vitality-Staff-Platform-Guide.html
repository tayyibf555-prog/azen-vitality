What the platform does, in one minute

Your platform sits on top of Dentally, the system the practice already uses. It quietly does a lot of the chasing for you, so patients get booked in and nothing slips through the cracks. Your job is to keep an eye on it and step in where a human touch is needed.

1

It reads Dentally

Appointments, recalls, treatment plans and balances, all live from your existing system.

2

It spots the work

Who is due a recall, who never came back, who might not turn up, who has just enquired.

3

It writes the message

A friendly, on-brand text or email, ready to go, or waiting for you to approve.

4

It handles the reply

The patient replies, the AI agent answers, books them in, and hands anything tricky to you.

i
Think of it as a very fast, very organised assistant sitting next to you. It never gives clinical advice, and anything it is unsure about (a clinical question, a complaint) it passes straight to a person.

A simple habit

Your morning routine

If you do these four things at the start of each shift, you will stay on top of everything.

  1. Open Home and read the Next tasks list. Start working from the top.
  2. Glance at today's diary for anyone still showing amber (not yet confirmed) and any gaps.
  3. Open Conversations and reply to anything the AI has handed to a person.
  4. Check Notifications for new enquiries or new patient forms that came in overnight.
✓
That is it. The automatic systems handle the reminders, recalls and follow-ups in the background, you just handle the human bits and the exceptions.

Task queue Home

What it is: instead of a messy to-do board, this hands you the single most important job to do next, then the next. New enquiries get a fast first touch, then spaced follow-ups before anyone goes cold.

How to use it

  1. Deal with the task on top.
  2. Mark it done, and the next one appears.
  3. Trust the order, the priority is already worked out for you.

Notifications & Daily brief Home

What they are: Notifications gathers anything that needs attention now, a new enquiry, a no-show risk, a new patient form. The Daily brief is a short morning summary: who to chase, gaps to fill, no-show risks, and high-value patients arriving today.

How to use it

  1. Glance at Notifications through the day, it is your alerts bell.
  2. Read the Daily brief each morning to see the day's priorities.
Working in the background

Winning back patients

These systems find patients who are due back, have drifted away, or have unfinished treatment, and gently bring them in. Mostly they run themselves, you review the worklists and approve or add a personal touch where it helps.

Recall concierge Patients menu

What it does: reads the dentist and hygienist recall dates the clinicians set in Dentally, and messages those patients to rebook, by text or email, respecting who has agreed to be contacted.

How to use it

  1. Open the list to see who is due or overdue a recall.
  2. Read the draft message and approve it, or let it send on its schedule.
  3. When a patient replies, the booking agent takes over to find them a time.

Reactivation Patients menu

What it does: finds patients who quietly stopped coming, and treatment plans that were started but never finished, and invites them back. It never contacts anyone marked as left the practice.

How to use it

  1. Review the worklist, sorted by how much each patient is worth winning back.
  2. Approve the friendly message, or let it run automatically.
  3. Replies flow into your inbox and the agent books them in.

Treatment Coordinator Patients menu

What it does: finds treatment a patient accepted but has not completed (a crown, implants, Invisalign), ranks it by value, re-presents finance, follows up, and books the next step. This is often the highest-value screen.

How to use it

  1. Open the list, the biggest opportunities are at the top.
  2. Review the suggested follow-up message.
  3. Approve it, or call a high-value patient personally for a warmer touch.

No-show defence Patients menu

What it does: automatically confirms upcoming appointments so fewer people miss them. It texts “are you still coming?” two days before, a reminder the day before, and a final nudge a few hours before. Patients most likely to miss are handled first. If someone cancels, the freed slot is offered to a waitlist so the gap gets filled.

Bringing new patients in

New patient enquiries

These screens capture new interest and turn it into booked patients quickly. Responding fast makes a huge difference to whether someone books.

Smile Assessment Growth menu

What it does: a short, friendly quiz on the website or an advert. It scores how ready and well-suited each enquiry is, and fast-tracks the keen ones to booking. It only ever judges interest and fit, never anything clinical.

How to use it

  1. Enquiries appear in your worklist with a score.
  2. Hot enquiries are contacted immediately by the booking agent.
  3. Pick up any that need a human follow-up.

Speed-to-lead Growth menu

What it does: the moment a new enquiry arrives, it reaches out within about 30 seconds, by text, email or WhatsApp, and records how quickly you responded.

How to use it

  1. Let it make the instant first contact automatically.
  2. Check the worklist to see who has replied and pick up the conversation if needed.

Onboarding Patients / Growth

What it does: a smart, branded new-patient form (at a link like /onboard/vitality) that collects contact details, a brief medical history, documents and consent, one step at a time. Completed forms land here for you to review and register.

How to use it

  1. Send the form link to a new patient (the agents can text it automatically too).
  2. When they submit, it appears in your submissions list.
  3. Review it and register the patient.
The conversations

The AI messaging agents

This is where every patient conversation lives. The AI agents handle most of it, and you can step in and take over any chat whenever you want.

Conversations Messages menu

What it is: one shared inbox for every patient conversation, across text, WhatsApp, after-hours calls and the automatic systems, grouped per person. Read anything, and jump in to reply yourself at any point.

  1. Open any conversation to read the full history.
  2. Let the agent continue, or press take over to reply in your own words.
  3. Everything respects consent and opt-outs. During the trial, replies are in test mode (see Good to know).

Booking agent & WhatsApp agent Messages menu

What they do: the two-way AI assistants. When a patient texts (or messages on WhatsApp), the agent recognises them from their number, answers questions, books, reschedules or cancels, sends reminders, and hands anything clinical, any complaint, or anything it is unsure about straight to a person. One works over normal text, one over WhatsApp.

How to use it

  1. Mostly it runs itself, patients simply text your number and get looked after.
  2. Anything handed to a human appears in Conversations for you to answer.
i
The agent never gives clinical or dental advice, and never mentions funding types. It sticks to booking, reminders, simple questions and friendly help, exactly as a great receptionist would.

After-hours capture Messages menu

What it does: catches calls the practice misses out of hours (or during a genuine rush) and books them, so enquiries that arrive when the phone is unattended are not lost.

How to use it

  1. Leave it on to capture missed calls automatically.
  2. Review anything captured the next morning in the worklist.
Your access

What you won't see (and that's fine)

Your login is set up for running the practice day to day. The owners have a few extra screens for the business, money and setup side. You do not need these to do your job, and not seeing them is completely normal.

Owner-only areas (not on your menu):

  • Business figures — ROI and written business reports.
  • Advertising — the Facebook and Instagram ad builder.
  • Compliance — CQC and GDC readiness, audits, policies and training.
  • Staff rota — building and sending the staff schedule.
  • Settings and on/off switches — connecting services and turning systems on or off.
i
If you ever need something from one of those areas, or think a system should be turned on or off, just ask an owner or the practice manager.
Please read

Good to know

!
Test mode until the practice goes live. While things are being set up, the platform is in a safe test mode. It does everything as normal, spots the work and drafts the messages, but it does not actually send texts or emails to patients yet. Nothing goes to a patient until the practice switches this on.
i
Some figures are sample data for now. Where a screen shows a small “Sample” label, those numbers are placeholders for the trial. They are replaced with real figures once the live data is fully connected.
✓
It is safe with patients. Every message respects who has agreed to be contacted and who has opted out. If a patient replies STOP, they are removed. The AI never gives clinical advice and always hands difficult or sensitive messages to a person. It reads Dentally, it does not change anything in it by accident.
Support

Getting help

If anything is unclear, or a screen is not doing what you expect, tell the practice manager or an owner. They can get it changed or reach the team that built it.

A few quick reminders

  • Sign in with your own email and password.
  • Start each shift on Home and work the tasks from the top.
  • Check Conversations for anything handed to a person.
  • A shorter menu than the owners is normal, you have everything you need.
  • Nothing sends to patients until the practice switches on live mode.
