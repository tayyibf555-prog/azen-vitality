What your platform does, in one minute

Your Vitality Dental platform sits on top of Dentally, the software the practice already uses. It quietly watches for opportunities and does the chasing for you, so patients get booked in and nothing slips through the cracks.

1

It reads Dentally

Appointments, recalls, treatment plans and balances, all live from your existing system.

2

It spots the work

Who is due a recall, who never came back, who has unfinished treatment, who might not show.

3

It writes the message

A friendly, on-brand text or email, ready for you to approve, or sent automatically.

4

It handles the reply

The patient replies, the AI agent answers, books them in, and updates your platform.

i
You are always in control. Every automatic system has an on/off switch, messages can be set to need your approval first, and anything the AI is unsure about (a clinical question, a complaint) is handed straight to a human.

How to use it

  1. Glance at Notifications through the day, it is your alerts bell.
  2. Read the Daily brief each morning to see your priorities in one place.
Automatic, working in the background

Winning back patients

This is where the platform earns its keep. These systems find patients who are due back, have drifted away, or have unfinished treatment, and gently bring them in. Each one shows you a worklist and, where you like, drafts the message for you to approve.

Recall concierge Patients menu

What it does: reads the exact dentist and hygienist recall dates your clinicians set in Dentally, and messages those patients to rebook, by text or email, respecting who has agreed to be contacted.

How to use it

  1. Open the list to see who is due or overdue a recall.
  2. Read the draft message the AI has prepared and either approve it or let it send on its schedule.
  3. When a patient replies, the booking agent takes over to find them a time.

Reactivation Patients menu

What it does: finds patients who quietly stopped coming (lapsed), and treatment plans that were started but never finished, and invites them back. It is careful, it never contacts someone marked as left the practice, and it leaves the very old, cold records alone.

How to use it

  1. Review the worklist of lapsed patients and stalled treatment, sorted by how much they are worth winning back.
  2. Approve the friendly “we would love to see you again” message, or let it run automatically.
  3. Replies flow into your inbox and the agent books them in.

Treatment Coordinator Patients menu

What it does: the highest-value system. It finds treatment a patient accepted but has not completed (a crown, implants, Invisalign), ranks it by value, re-presents finance options, follows up, and books the next step.

How to use it

  1. Open the list, the biggest opportunities are at the top.
  2. Review the suggested follow-up message and any finance reminder.
  3. Approve it, or hand a high-value case to a coordinator to call personally.

No-show defence Patients menu

What it does: automatically confirms upcoming appointments so fewer people miss them. It texts “are you still coming?” two days before, a reminder the day before, and a final nudge a few hours before. Patients most likely to miss are handled first. If someone says no or cancels, the freed slot is offered to a waitlist so the gap gets filled.

Bringing new patients in

New patient enquiries

These screens capture new interest and turn it into booked patients quickly, because responding fast makes an enormous difference to whether someone books.

Smile Assessment Growth menu

What it does: a short, friendly quiz you can put on your website or link from an advert. It asks a few questions, scores how ready and well-suited each enquiry is, and fast-tracks the keen ones straight to booking while nurturing the rest. It only ever judges interest and fit, never anything clinical.

How to use it

  1. Owners set up a campaign link to share (on the website, social media, or an advert).
  2. Enquiries come in and appear in your worklist with a score.
  3. Hot enquiries are contacted immediately by the booking agent.

Speed-to-lead Growth menu

What it does: the moment a new enquiry arrives, it reaches out within about 30 seconds, by text, email or WhatsApp, and records how quickly you responded. Replying in seconds rather than minutes dramatically improves how many enquiries turn into patients.

How to use it

  1. Let it make the instant first contact automatically.
  2. Check the worklist to see who has replied and pick up the conversation if needed.
  3. Use the response-time labels to spot any enquiry that slipped through.

Onboarding Patients / Growth

What it does: a smart, branded new-patient form (at a link like /onboard/vitality) that collects contact details, a brief medical history, documents and consent, one step at a time. Completed forms land here for the team to review and register.

How to use it

  1. Send the form link to a new patient (the agents can text it automatically too).
  2. When they submit, it appears in your submissions list.
  3. Review it and register the patient. You can create different forms for different purposes.

Meta Ads Owners only

What it does: plan, build and track Facebook and Instagram campaigns without an agency. It gives you ready-to-launch templates, AI-written ad copy, a step-by-step launch guide, performance figures, and a library of proven dental ads. It follows UK advertising rules for dentistry.

How to use it

  1. Pick a template and let the AI draft the wording.
  2. Follow the launch guide to put it live on your own Meta account.
  3. Come back to see how it is performing.
The conversations

The AI messaging agents

This is where every patient conversation lives. The AI agents can handle most of it, and you can step in and take over any chat whenever you want.

Conversations Messages menu

What it is: one shared inbox for every patient conversation, across text, WhatsApp, after-hours calls and the automatic systems, grouped per person. You can read anything and jump in to reply yourself at any point.

How to use it

  1. Open any conversation to read the full history.
  2. Let the agent continue, or press take over to reply in your own words.
  3. Everything respects consent and opt-outs. During the trial, replies are in test mode (see Good to know).

Booking agent & WhatsApp agent Messages menu

What they do: these are the two-way AI assistants. When a patient texts (or messages on WhatsApp), the agent recognises them from their phone number, answers questions, books, reschedules or cancels appointments, sends reminders, and hands anything clinical, any complaint, or anything it is unsure about straight to a human. The two agents behave the same way, one over normal text, one over WhatsApp.

How to use it

  1. Mostly it runs itself, patients simply text your number and get looked after.
  2. On these screens you can see how it is set up and review its conversations.
  3. Anything handed to a human appears in Conversations for you to answer.
i
The agent never gives clinical or dental advice, and never mentions funding types. It sticks to booking, reminders, simple questions and friendly help, exactly as a great receptionist would.

After-hours capture Messages menu

What it does: catches calls the practice misses out of hours (or during a genuine rush) and books them, so enquiries that arrive when the phone is unattended are not lost. It is deliberately only for missed and overflow calls, not a replacement receptionist during opening hours.

How to use it

  1. Leave it on to capture missed calls automatically.
  2. Review anything captured the next morning in the worklist.
For owners

Owner tools

These live in the Operations menu and a couple of business screens. They are for the practice owners, the practice manager does not see them.

System controls Owners only

What it is: your master on/off panel. Every automatic system (recall, reactivation, no-show defence, the agents, and the rest) has a single switch. Turning one off is a proper stop: it hides that module and halts all its behind-the-scenes work, so nothing sends until you switch it back on. This is your safety valve.

Settings & Getting started Owners only

What they are: Settings is where your services connect (Dentally, text and email sending, reviews, Meta) and where you set whether messages send for real or stay in test mode. Getting started is a plain checklist of what we need from the practice to go fully live, including the few items that must be in place before any real patient messages can send.

How to use it

  1. Work through the Getting started checklist with us.
  2. In Settings, check each service shows as connected.
  3. When you are ready, we switch messaging from test mode to live together.

ROI, Reports & Ask the brain Owners only

What they are: ROI shows how your spend turns into leads, bookings and revenue, with your cost per new patient. Reports writes plain-English weekly and monthly business reviews with recommendations. Ask the brain lets you ask the platform questions about your practice and get grounded answers.

How to use it

  1. Open ROI to see the money picture at a glance.
  2. Read the latest Report for a written summary and suggestions.
  3. Type a question into Ask the brain whenever you want a quick answer.

Compliance, Staff rota & USPs Owners only

What they are: Compliance organises your CQC and GDC readiness, an audit calendar, policy library and staff training matrix (it is an organiser and decision-support, not legal advice). Staff rota generates shifts from your opening hours and staff availability and texts each person their shifts. USPs is where owners list the practice's selling points, which the AI agents weave into their messages.

How to use it

  1. Use Compliance to keep audits, policies and training in one place.
  2. Set your rules in Staff rota and let it build and send the schedule.
  3. Keep your USPs short and truthful, the agents will use them.

Please read

Good to know before go-live

!
Test mode until we go live. While we set up, the platform is in a safe test mode. It does everything as normal, spots the work and drafts the messages, but it does not actually send texts or emails to patients yet. When the practice is ready and the checklist is complete, we turn on real sending together. Nothing goes to a patient before you say so.
i
Some figures are sample data for now. Where a screen shows a small “Sample” label, those numbers are placeholders for the trial, so you can see how the screen looks. They are replaced with your real figures once your live data is fully connected.
✓
It is safe with your patients. Every message respects who has agreed to be contacted and who has opted out. If a patient replies STOP, they are removed. The AI never gives clinical advice and always hands difficult or sensitive messages to a human. It reads your Dentally, it does not change anything in it by accident.
i
It works around the clock. The background systems (confirmations, recalls, follow-ups) keep running 24 hours a day, even when no one is logged in and the practice is closed.
Support

Getting help

If anything is unclear, or you want a screen changed, a login added, or a system turned on or off, just tell us. This platform is built for your practice and we adjust it as you need.

A few quick reminders

  • Sign in at your web address with your own email and password.
  • Start each day on Home and work the tasks from the top.
  • A shorter menu for the practice manager is normal.
